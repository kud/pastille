Pastille is a keyboard you switch to only when you want to paste something. Tap a saved snippet, a sticker, a recent image or something you just copied and it goes straight into the text field, then Pastille hands you back to your usual keyboard.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Your own snippets, sorted into folders</li>
<li>Stickers, inserted as images where the app accepts them</li>
<li>Recent images from the folders you choose, such as Screenshots or Camera</li>
<li>The keyboard's Clipboard view: your last copies, ready to paste or keep as a snippet</li>
<li>Share text or an image from any app into Pastille to keep it</li>
<li>Send a snippet or sticker with Quick Share, or with "Send to another phone"</li>
<li>Show a snippet as a QR code for another phone's camera</li>
<li>Sync with a file you choose, in Drive, pCloud or on the phone</li>
<li>A Bin, so a deleted snippet can come back</li>
<li>A Quick Settings tile</li>
<li>Return to your usual keyboard after pasting</li>
<li>Themes and panel heights to suit your phone</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
Pastille has no internet permission. Your snippets, stickers and images stay on your phone unless you export, share or back them up yourself.
