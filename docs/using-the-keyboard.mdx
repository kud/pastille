## The toolbar

While you browse, the toolbar has the tools at the start and the tabs at the end, where your thumb lands:

| Control | Tap | Long press |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard button | Switch back to your usual keyboard | Open Android's keyboard picker, to choose any other keyboard |
| Settings (gear) | Open the keyboard's own settings panel | |
| `+` on Snippets | **New snippet** | |
| `+` on Stickers | **Add stickers** | |
| Clear on Clipboard | **Clear clipboard** | |

## The four tabs

The tabs sit in this order: **Clipboard**, **Snippets**, **Stickers**, **Images**. The keyboard reopens on the tab you last used. Any tab can be hidden under **Show in the keyboard**, but at least one of Snippets, Stickers and Images always stays on.

### Clipboard

The last 10 things you copied while Pastille's keyboard or app was open, kept for an hour, in memory only. Passwords and copies marked as sensitive are skipped.

- **Tap** a clip to paste it.
- **Long press** for **Paste**, **Save as snippet** and **Remove**.

Turning Clipboard off under **Show in the keyboard** stops the recording and clears what was kept.

### Snippets

Your saved snippets, as tiles. If you have folders, a row of chips above them lets you pick **All** or one folder, and ends with a **Reorder** chip: hold and drag to reorder snippets and folders right in the keyboard.

- **Tap** a snippet to insert it.
- **Long press** to open its actions: **Edit in app**, **Make sticker** (image snippets only) and **Delete**, plus a folder choice when you have folders.

### Stickers

Your own stickers, added from the photo picker or the share sheet. Transparent PNG, WebP and animated GIF or WebP are kept as they are.

- **Tap** a sticker to insert it.
- **Long press** for **Edit in app**, **Move to snippets** and **Delete**.

### Images

Recent images from the folders you choose under **Image folders** (Screenshots, Camera, Downloads and so on). This tab needs photo access.

- **Tap** an image to insert it.
- **Long press** to preview it larger, with an **Insert** button.

## How inserting works

Text goes straight into the field. An image is inserted as an image where the app accepts one; where it doesn't, Pastille copies it to the clipboard instead and says "Copied, long-press to paste".

After a snippet or an image goes in, Pastille can switch back to your usual keyboard. Fresh installs return after a snippet but stay after an image, so you can paste several images in a row. Both are separate switches, covered in [Settings](/projects/pastille/docs/settings#keyboard).

## Adding a snippet from the keyboard

Tap `+` on the Snippets tab to open **New snippet**, with three sources:

- **From text field** (or **From selection** when text is selected): saves what is in the field you're typing in. Unavailable when the field is empty, is a sensitive field, or the app doesn't share its text.
- **From clipboard**: saves what you last copied, unless it is marked sensitive.
- **Write in Pastille app**: opens the app's editor.

When you have folders, you can pick which one the new snippet goes into.

## The keyboard's settings panel

The gear opens a short panel with the settings you'd want mid-paste: **Look**, **Height** (or **Height in landscape**), **Show in the keyboard**, **Return after a snippet**, **Return after an image**, **Image folders**, and **All settings**, which opens the app's full settings.

## The Quick Settings sheet

The Quick Settings tile opens a sheet of your snippets, stickers and recent images over the current app, with search. It copies rather than inserts:

- **Tap** an item to copy it to the clipboard.
- **Long press** for its actions: **Edit in app**, **Delete** and **Share** for a snippet, **Copy** and **Share** for an image.

The keyboard button in the sheet's header opens Android's keyboard picker.

## In the app

The app is where you write and organise. A few things live only there:

- **Search** looks in titles, text and `#tags`.
- A snippet's menu offers **Move to folder**, **Share**, **Show QR code** (text snippets only, shown full screen for another phone's camera) and **Send to another phone**, which shares it as a file the other phone can import.
- Deleted snippets go to the **Bin**, where they stay for 30 days before being removed for good.
