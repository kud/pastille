Pastille is an open-source Android keyboard you switch to only when you want to paste: your own snippets, recent clipboard items, stickers and recent images are a tap away, and once one goes into the text field Pastille can switch straight back to the keyboard you type with.

It needs Android 9 or later, and keeps everything on the phone unless you export, share or sync it yourself.
